## 1. Information Collection and Use

**NivaTools: Quick Convert** is designed as an offline-first productivity tool. 

* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or contact list.
